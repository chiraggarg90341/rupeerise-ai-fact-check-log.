RupeeRise (Bengaluru, India)

Market Opportunity

India is experiencing an unprecedented convergence of real-time digital payments and automated credit underwriting. The domestic digital lending market has experienced a 13-fold expansion over the past five years, rising from ₹15,000 crore in FY21 to ₹2.2 lakh crore in FY26. This rapid expansion is supercharged by the world's largest real-time payment network, the Unified Payments Interface (UPI). In the single financial year of FY25–26, UPI processed an extraordinary 24,162 crore annual transactions, capturing roughly 49% of global real-time payment volumes and onboarding a staggering 55.49 crore unique users.
For a nimble digital-first lender like RupeeRise, this ecosystem provides high-frequency data and friction-free repayment rails. As noted by Nandan Nilekani, Non-Executive Chairman of Infosys and architect of India's digital public infrastructure, "India is moving from being data-poor to data-rich, and this data wealth will enable democratised, instant credit access for millions."

Regulatory Guardrails & Compliance-First Execution

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has introduced robust frameworks to ensure systemic stability while fostering sustainable financial innovation. RupeeRise is built from the ground up to comply strictly with the core pillars of the Reserve Bank of India (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025:
• Direct Fund Flows: To ensure transparency and prevent the pooling of capital by intermediaries, all loan disbursals and repayments must execute directly between the bank account of the Regulated Entity (RE) and the borrower, fully eliminating third-party pool accounts.
• Credit Enhancements Cap: Under the default loss protection parameters, any Default Loss Guarantee (DLG) agreements structured between a Fintech platform and its balance-sheet partners are strictly capped at 5% of the underlying loan portfolio to mitigate systemic risk.

The RupeeRise Advantage

By embedding directly into UPI-driven transaction histories, RupeeRise bypasses traditional underwriting bottlenecks to accurately capture customer journeys.
